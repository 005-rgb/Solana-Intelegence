---
name: Project traction evidence
description: Durable constraints for Phase 3B project reality and traction verification.
---

Project Quality caps may lift only when the evidence adapter supplies fresh, as-of, verified records covering product reality and economic/user activity from at least two independent sources. Market price, volume, liquidity, or boosts are not substitutes for product traction.

**Why:** The live market provider supplies discovery and market activity, but it does not verify product usage, users, revenue, developer activity, or integrations. Counting those fields as project traction would create false fundamental confidence.

**How to apply:** Persist every evaluation beside its token observation with source lineage, freshness, unknown dimensions, and trend sample quality. Exclude stale, future, invalid, or insufficient-source evidence and keep the explicit quality cap.
